French frequency dictionary attribution
=======================================

Derived from FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave.
Source: https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords
Original corpus: OpenSubtitles 2018 frequency data.

Content license: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)

Changes made for this application:
- filtered symbols, corpus noise, explicit terms, and frequent proper-name pollution;
- normalized apostrophe variants and added accent-insensitive lookup metadata;
- retained French display accents and curated complete contractions;
- split and ranked the resulting dictionary for local keyboard suggestions.
